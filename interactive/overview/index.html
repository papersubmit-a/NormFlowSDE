NormFlowSDE estimates states conditional on observations

Move the time point t with the slider, the step buttons or a click in panel (c), or press play. The trailing window of the last o measurements (box in a, shaded in c) moves forward with t; the encoder turns it into a new context ct, which conditions the update from zt to zt+Δt (b, boxed in c); the one-step distribution in (b) changes with t and widens or narrows with the band in (c). Measurements after t (faded) are not used, so the estimate is causal. Schematic: the drawing uses o = 4 (the trained models use 10), and all curves are illustrative.
