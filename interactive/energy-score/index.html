Energy score versus entropy term

A. Ensemble spread c (reference x ~ 𝒩(0, Id), members z = c ε, ε ~ 𝒩(0, Id))

E‖z − x‖ ½ E‖z − z′‖ sum: energy score E‖z − x‖ − ½ E‖z − z′‖ entropy term H(𝒩(0, c²Id)) (lower plot)

B. The learned scale sφ recalibrates a miscalibrated spread (energy score of s·c0)

Toy setting: reference x ~ 𝒩(0, Id) and ensemble members z = c ε with ε ~ 𝒩(0, Id); all curves are closed-form expectations, not trained models. Move the spread c: the energy score (the sum, Section 3.2.3) is minimised at the calibrated spread c = 1 in every dimension d, whereas the entropy term of Stage 1 grows with c, so on its own it favours contraction (Appendix B). Panel B multiplies a miscalibrated spread c0 by a scale s, as the learned scale sφ of Stage 2 does; the energy score is minimised at s = 1/c0, where the product is calibrated again.
