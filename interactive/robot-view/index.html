Joint-space filtering distributions of NormFlowSDE (seed 0) on the two robot datasets, replayed on the robots' kinematic models (no physics is simulated). The grey arm is the reference state, the orange arm the posterior median, and the translucent arms are individual posterior draws; the timeline shows the selected joint with its 5–95 % band, the measurements, and the band width. The eleven test windows are those at the 0th, 10th, …, 90th percentiles of the per-window RMSE plus the worst window. REASSEMBLE's state has no gripper coordinate, so its fingers are drawn open.
