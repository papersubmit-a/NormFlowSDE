Joint-space filtering distributions of NormFlowSDE (seed 0) on the two robot datasets, replayed on the robots' kinematic models (no physics is simulated). Show the reference state with the posterior draws as translucent ghost arms, or with the posterior median. The panel on the right plots the end-effector trajectories over the whole window in a chosen projection; the timeline shows the selected joint with its 5–95 % band, the measurements, and the band width. The eleven test windows are those at the 0th, 10th, …, 90th percentiles of the per-window RMSE plus the worst window. REASSEMBLE's state has no gripper coordinate, so its fingers are drawn open.
